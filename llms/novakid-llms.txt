# Novakid

> Novakid is an online English language school for children aged roughly 4-12, founded in 2017
> by Max Azarov and Dmitry Malin. It delivers 25-minute one-to-one and small-group video lessons
> with native and near-native speaking teachers through a proprietary gamified virtual classroom,
> on a CEFR-aligned curriculum, sold directly to parents as a subscription across Europe, Latin
> America, the Middle East and Asia.
